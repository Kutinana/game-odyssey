大力，神！Hercules

游戏背景

你身体矮小，但生有一双孔武有力的手臂。你对自己的身体很不满意，于是你决定收集金苹果，然后向大力神许愿拥有正常的身体。利用你的强壮手臂，打败敌人，收集金苹果吧！

玩法

使用AD键左右移动，移动鼠标控制手臂实现跳跃、攻击等操作。使用E键抓取物品。收集4颗金苹果即可取得胜利！

Background

You have a small body but possess powerful arms. Dissatisfied with your physique, you decide to collect golden apples and then make a wish to Hercules to have a normal body. Utilize your strong arms to defeat enemies and collect golden apples!

Gameplay

Use the AD keys to move left and right, move the mouse to control the arms for jumping, attacking, and other actions. Use the E key to grab objects. Collect 4 golden apples to achieve victory!
