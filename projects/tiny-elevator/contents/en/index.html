Introduction

You are a TINY CREATURE, who is an ELEVATOR coordinator in your company: “Hell’o World”. Your boss is strict with the company’s employees. You need to help them escape from the company (exit is the green floor) after work ASAP, with your control over the elevators. All of this happens on a scientist’s desk. He will disturb you from completing your work.

Operations

Press [W], [S] to move the ELEVATOR 1 Press [UpArrow], [DownArrow] to move the ELEVATOR 1

Press [RightArrow] to open/close door of ELEVATOR 1 and get people in/stop Press [D] to open/close door of ELEVATOR 2 and get people in/stop

ELEVATOR 2 unlocks at level 6, you can also choose the hardest 2 player mode, and compete with your friend (if you have one :p).
